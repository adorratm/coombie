# Prompt 4 – Zombie & boss variety

Act as a senior enemy AI and encounter designer.

Design an advanced zombie enemy ecosystem.

Create:

1. Basic zombies
2. Fast zombies
3. Tank zombies
4. Explosive zombies
5. Ranged zombies
6. Elite enemies
7. Mini bosses
8. Main bosses

For each enemy define:

- Health
- Movement speed
- Damage
- Attack behavior
- Special abilities
- Spawn conditions
- Difficulty scaling
- Visual identity

Boss encounters should require different strategies.

Avoid enemies that only differ in health and damage.
