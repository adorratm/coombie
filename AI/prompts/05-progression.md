# Prompt 5 – Progression & retention

Act as a senior roguelike progression designer.

Design a deep progression system for my zombie survivor game.

Include:

- XP and level progression.
- Randomized upgrade selection.
- Weapon evolution.
- Passive upgrades.
- Character unlocks.
- Permanent upgrades.
- Achievement system.
- Daily and weekly challenges.
- Difficulty modifiers.
- Meta progression.

Every upgrade must have a meaningful gameplay impact.

Implement a synergy system where combinations of weapons and passive abilities create unique builds.

Focus on replayability and long-term engagement.
