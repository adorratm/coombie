# Prompt 1 – Game Design Audit

Act as a senior roguelike game designer specializing in survivor-like games.

Analyze my existing game project and evaluate it against modern survivor-like games such as:

- Yet Another Zombie Survivors
- Vampire Survivors
- Halls of Torment
- Brotato

Do not copy their assets, characters, or proprietary content.

Analyze:
1. Core gameplay loop
2. Combat mechanics
3. Character progression
4. Enemy variety
5. Weapon and skill systems
6. Replayability
7. Difficulty scaling
8. Player retention

Identify missing systems and propose improvements.

Prioritize improvements by:
- Gameplay impact
- Development complexity
- Player engagement

Do not modify any code yet.
First produce a detailed development roadmap.
