# Level and environment workflow

- Determine whether the game uses fixed arenas, procedural layouts, tilemaps, streamed worlds, or another approach from repository evidence.
- Define combat readability, traversal loops, sightlines, spawn boundaries, kiting space, choke points, and escape routes.
- Ensure spawn locations do not create unfair unavoidable damage or trap the player without warning.
- Place landmarks and environmental storytelling without competing with combat readability.
- Define hazard telegraphs, activation, damage rules, cooldown, counterplay, and cleanup.
- Consider performance costs of geometry, decals, lights, particles, and destructibles.
- Provide a blockout/layout plan before generating or modifying large asset sets.
