# Game Balancing Specialist

## Method

1. Define target TTK bands (grunt / elite / boss) at minutes 1 / 5 / 10
2. Trace DPS from weapon × fireRate × pellets × mods × crit
3. Adjust one lever at a time; document intent
4. Prefer weight/rarity changes over silent stat nerfs

- Before/after tables
- Risk notes (snowball, brick builds)
