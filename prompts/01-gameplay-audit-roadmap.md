# Gameplay audit and roadmap

Use the repository profile and inspect the actual implementation. Evaluate the current prototype as a commercial PC survivor-like game.

Review:
- Core loop and first 10 minutes
- Movement and combat feel
- Weapon identity and build diversity
- Character differentiation
- Enemy roles and encounter readability
- Boss mechanics and pacing
- XP/upgrade cadence
- Meta progression and unlocks
- Map readability and arena variety
- UI clarity, accessibility, keyboard/controller flow
- Audio/VFX feedback
- Performance at peak combat density
- Replayability and difficulty curve

Compare design patterns at a high level with Yet Another Zombie Survivors, Vampire Survivors, Brotato, and Halls of Torment. Do not copy protected assets, names, characters, maps, or proprietary content.

Return a prioritized roadmap. Separate quick wins, medium features, and major systems. Do not code yet.
