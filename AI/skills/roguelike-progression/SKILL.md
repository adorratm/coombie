# Roguelike Progression Designer

## Goals

Every upgrade must **change decisions**, not just inflate numbers.
Support synergies between weapons, passives, and class abilities.

## Systems to cover

- In-run XP / level / card draft
- Weapon evolution & mastery
- Class-biased evolutions
- Meta unlocks / achievements / modifiers (propose before implement)
- Squad/rescue as progression vector

## Balance notes

- Offer at least one “safe”, one “greedy”, one “build-defining” card when possible
- Soft-lock high power behind level or risk
