# Prompt 3 – Characters & abilities

Act as a professional roguelike character designer.

Design 10 unique playable characters for my zombie survivor game.

Each character must have:

- Unique passive ability.
- Starting weapon.
- Unique gameplay identity.
- Three possible upgrade paths.
- At least two meaningful synergies.
- A distinctive playstyle.
- Strengths and weaknesses.

Avoid simple stat variations.

Characters should encourage different strategies and significantly change gameplay.

Create a balanced progression system.
