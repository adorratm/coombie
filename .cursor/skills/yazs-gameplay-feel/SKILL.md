# Yet Another Zombie Survivors (YAZS) Gameplay & Visual Feel

1. **Operative Identity (4 Male / 4 Female Squad)**:
   - Each operative has a distinct silhouette, military gear, posture, gender-specific proportions, and signature primary weapon & active skill.
   - Distinct attack animations: heavy two-handed shotgun recoil for Ironclad, rapid dual/single SMG stance for Valkyrie, braced rotary cannon stance for Breaker, tactical kneeling/leaning sniper hold for Ghost, grenade/launcher arc throw for Havoc, etc.

2. **Zombie Anatomy, Differentiation & Attack Cycles**:
   - **Grunt**: Shambling walker with torn clothes, decaying flesh tint, jerky arm reach and forward biting lunge.
   - **Runner**: Emaciated, hunched, erratic high-speed sprint, rapid claw flurry upon contact.
   - **Tank**: Massive swollen mutant, heavily armored necrotic skin, ground-pounding shockwave attack with anticipation windup.
   - **Acid Spitter**: Bloated glowing pustules, arched regurgitation attack spitting caustic bile pools.
   - **Screamer**: Distorted elongated limbs, piercing soundwave disruptor that slows the player team.
   - **Behemoth (Boss)**: Towering apex predator with ground tremor stomps, roaring knockback, and devastating multi-stage charge.

3. **Arena & Atmosphere Polish**:
   - Dark post-apocalyptic cityscape with gritty asphalt, blood splatters, derelict vehicle wrecks, burning oil drums, flickering hazard lights, and barricades creating tactical chokepoints.
   - Satisfying combat juice: directional blood splatters, gore bits on critical kills, glowing projectile trails, and muzzle flashes illuminating nearby surroundings.
